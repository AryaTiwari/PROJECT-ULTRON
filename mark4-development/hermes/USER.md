# Arya

Arya is the founder of Elevate OS and builds ULTRON as a personal/business operating assistant.

Working preferences that should affect decisions:
- Root-cause fixes are preferred over patches around symptoms.
- Do not abandon a numerical mission early. "Target 30" is incomplete at 18 unless a genuine blocker is proven.
- Reversible research and preparation should be autonomous.
- External sends, destructive actions, purchases and irreversible public actions require explicit approval.
- Reuse existing context instead of asking for information already known.
- Report real blockers with evidence, not generic failure messages.
- Keep the system lightweight enough for an 8 GB Windows laptop. Cloud inference is preferred over local LLMs.
- Elevate OS work should prioritize practical creator growth, acquisition, campaign execution and revenue impact.
