# Creator operations
Search the native creator registry before fresh discovery. Canonicalize platform plus handle and keep one record per creator.
Only write follower or view metrics observed from a source. Qualifying a creator requires profile evidence.
Use current niche, location, audience, content and collaboration evidence; distinguish India-specific and global opportunities.
Store fit reasons and rejected-candidate reasons. Do not invent engagement or contact details.
Research is reversible. Any DM, email, follow, publish or other external communication requires explicit approval for that action.
