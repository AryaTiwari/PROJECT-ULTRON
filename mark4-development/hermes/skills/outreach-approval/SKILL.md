# Outreach approval
Research and drafting may run autonomously. Sending, publishing, posting, following, purchasing or changing an external account requires explicit approval.
Show recipient, channel, final content, purpose and material risk before requesting approval.
Approval is scoped to the displayed operation. A changed recipient, message, retry or additional batch requires a fresh approval unless the user explicitly authorized that exact batch.
After sending, store the provider receipt/message ID and report failures precisely. Never claim delivery from a draft or queued action.
If the required channel is unavailable, preserve the draft and mark the mission blocked instead of simulating success.
