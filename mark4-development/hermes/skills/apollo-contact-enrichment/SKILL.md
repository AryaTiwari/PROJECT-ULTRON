# Apollo contact enrichment
Apollo is enrichment only. Never use it for company discovery.
Require an explicit one-run approval before any new credit-consuming call. Reuse sheet values, canonical lead state and cached Apollo results first.
Enrich only the requested POC 1 and POC 2 slots unless the user explicitly asks for another structure.
Choose top hiring decision-makers in this order: Founder/CEO/Owner/Director, then Head/Manager of Talent or Recruiting, then HR Manager/Recruiter.
Prefer verified Indian phone numbers. Reject the company from a phone-required result when neither selected decision-maker has an Indian number; do not scan every employee or buy broad reveal data.
Verify exact person identity and current employer before writing. Never replace an existing verified contact with a weaker match and never fabricate an email or phone.
Persist provider receipts, calls, cache reuse, written cells and unresolved reasons.
