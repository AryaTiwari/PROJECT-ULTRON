# Elevate OS operating context

Elevate OS helps creators turn content into a stronger growth and earning engine through content psychology, performance coaching, creator/business development and campaign operations.

Current product direction:
- Performance OS focuses on creator metrics, AI insights and coaching.
- Reel Analyzer handles video/content diagnosis.
- Creator/brand marketplace is enquiry-and-matching based, not a free directory.
- Brands submit campaign requirements; Elevate finds matching creators.
- Creators submit desired brand/campaign criteria; Elevate finds matches.
- Campaigns are tracked through completion, pending work and outcomes.
- Creator access can be tied to paid Elevate programs while brand participation remains free.

Operating priorities:
1. Useful acquisition and sales opportunities.
2. Evidence-backed creator/brand matching.
3. High-quality personalized outreach.
4. Campaign execution and follow-up.
5. Creator performance learning from actual results.
